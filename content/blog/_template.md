## Videos

Two ways to add a video:

1. **A video post**: put `video: <link>` in the frontmatter above. The player goes at the top,
   and the post shows up under 🎥 Videos on the blog with a thumbnail.
2. **A video inside a post**: put the link on its own line, anywhere in the text:

https://youtu.be/VIDEO_ID

Works with YouTube (including Shorts), Vimeo, Loom, and files in `public/videos/` (e.g. `/videos/demo.mp4`).
